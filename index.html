RACE RULES
  • Laps: follow the arrows and cross the checkered line each lap. Driving backwards never counts.
  • Nitro: hold your Shift key to boost. It drains the blue meter and slowly recharges when released.
  • Off-road: grass, sand and gravel slow you down hard. Steer back to the tarmac.
  • Drifting: brake into corners or combine nitro with steering to slide — smoke and skid marks show it.
  • Stuck? Press Q (P1) or / (P2) to be placed back on track. It also happens automatically if you stall.
  • Game keys: Esc pause · R restart (pause or results) · M mute · Enter confirm.
  • Time Trial: cars ghost through each other — fastest total time wins; chase the medal times.
  • Grand Prix: 10 points for a win, 6 for second, 0 for not finishing. Most points after 3 races is champion.
